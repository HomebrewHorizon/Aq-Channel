# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We pledge to create a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Collaborate in good faith and support others.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Disruptive behavior that hinders productive collaboration.

## Enforcement
Violations will be addressed by the project maintainers, with actions including warnings, temporary suspension, or removal from the community.

---

By participating in Aq-Channel, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
